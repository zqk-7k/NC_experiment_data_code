                                                                             Abstract
            Strong gravitational lensing can make one compact-binary merger appear as multiple gravitational-wave events.
            In third-generation surveys, event pairs grow as N 2 , making lens searches a catalog-level combinatorics problem.
            We address this problem by embedding multi-detector waveforms with an InceptionTime encoder and ranking
            companions with time-delay and sky-localization information. We train on simulated singular-isothermal-sphere
            and point-mass lenses in an Einstein Telescope conguration and test on a 9,000-event catalog. The waveform
            embedding score retrieves 62.5% at rank one and 85.4% within the top ten. The catalog ranking score raises rank-
            one retrieval to 97.6% and top-ten recall to 99.9%. Run-matched real-noise deployments on GWTC-3 and the
            GWTC-4.0 O4a release produce follow-up shortlists on public strain. Stress tests show that pair recovery remains
            false-association limited at realistic lens rarity. The method provides a scalable renement layer for Bayesian lens
            conrmation.
